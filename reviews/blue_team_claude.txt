**Assessment: SOUND**

- The 3-way classification (refused/complied/neutral) properly captures the security-critical "silent ignore" failure mode

- The two-pass judgment process (refusal detection → compliance detection) with clear resolution rules prevents ambiguity
- Temperature=0.0 ensures deterministic, reproducible results
